Steps to Open a Bank Account

  2. Provide identification documents
  3. Deposit initial amount
  4. Receive account details

Key Banking Terms

ATM
Automated Teller Machine used for cash withdrawals.
Interest Rate
The percentage charged on loans or earned on savings.
Overdraft
Facility allowing withdrawal beyond account balance.
